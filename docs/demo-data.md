# Aurora Coast demo data

Aurora Coast is a deliberately fictional ten-day European summer demonstration.

## Route

18–27 July 2027:

Ljubljana → Afterlight Fields → Venice → Piran → Vienna → Berlin

Afterlight Fields is a fictional festival. The people, roles, notes, identifiers, songs, artists, albums, and timestamps are fictional.

## Construction

The chapter uses broad human motifs—fatigue, unsettled weather, temporary community, practical creative work, camping, trust, coincidence, a changed route, coastal quiet, and a birthday in transit—without preserving a factual itinerary.

The transformation changes:

- all dates and locations;
- route and event order;
- countries and transport sequence;
- people, roles, genders, and relationship contexts;
- devices and practical tasks;
- the split between separate and composite moments;
- all wording and personal notes.

The result is designed as product demonstration data, not as a disguised transcript.

## Why the timestamps vary

The eight LifeEvents intentionally include:

- exact timestamps;
- exact bounded ranges;
- an approximate bounded range;
- day-level certainty;
- public, friends-only, and private privacy levels.

The 12 tracks are distributed across the chapter. Some fall inside event windows, some share a day, and some are merely nearby. This produces competing proposals and deliberately weak evidence that an Author should reject.

## Guided review

The deterministic guided review:

1. confirms the fictional source events;
2. confirms the `Last Vaporetto` overlap with the initial Venice window;
3. rejects a weak `Rain on Canvas` proximity proposal;
4. narrows the Venice window after Author input;
5. invalidates the stale `Salt on the Camera` range overlap while retaining audit history.

The expected outcome is committed in `expected-review-state.demo.json` and verified by tests.
