# Three-location interaction script

Each location is simulated by default; no real coordinate appears in the public demo.

1. **OK Quarter** — show *Deep Space Cities: Shared Immersive Futures* and its festival source; show a selected City spatial fragment; reveal private fragment A; label the temporal relationship `inferred / ambiguous`; Accept makes the sentence authored.
2. **Hauptplatz** — show *Flood the Zone with Courage*; reveal private fragment B; expose that the City fixture has no used geometry; choose Refuse. The result remains: “These records overlap, but the proposed story is not mine.”
3. **Ars Electronica Center** — show *Hello Worlds!*; show private fragment C; revise the associative sentence. Original sources and transformations remain visible.

Participant experience: they see the three labelled source layers and ledger, hear no copyrighted audio, select a simulated place, and control a distinct Accept, Revise or Refuse action. Refusal can be a dimmed field, interrupted waveform, silence or crossed-out route in a later installation; here it is a visible crossed-out inference and exportable state.
