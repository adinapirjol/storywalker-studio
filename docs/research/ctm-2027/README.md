# CTM 2027 research notebook

This public folder contains blank, reusable research structures and the factual CTM tracker reflected in the Research Lab. It contains no personal notebook entries, real coordinates, playlist data, or Author decisions.

Verified 23 August 2026 against the official [Research Networking Day call](https://www.ctm-festival.de/festival-2027/open-calls/research-networking-day-2027) and [CTM 2027 theme](https://www.ctm-festival.de/festival-2027/theme) pages. Confirmed facts and working inferences are deliberately separated in [the tracker](tracker.md).

The working direction is **Refusal Is an Editorial Act: Who Gets to Author the Memory?** It remains provisional until 15 September 2026.

The provisional Linz experiment extends the primary question: **What happens when public place-data and private behavioural traces are allowed to propose autobiographical meaning, but not to author it?** Its records and blank reflection structures are in [`../linz-2026`](../linz-2026/README.md); only synthetic public fixtures are committed.

Use the templates as local copies. Personal entries belong in ignored private data, not this directory.
