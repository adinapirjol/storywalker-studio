## Priorities for Listening Again

Start with Notari, Echeverría and Poulsen for source conditions, present-time relistening and contestable comfort. Use Dias to review whether text equivalents impose their own interpretation. Martins sharpens the distinction between platform extraction and our reuse of its traces. Costa helps frame spatial sound as an artistic decision rather than a technical achievement. Rusu and Kascak offer situated regional comparisons without licensing sweeping regional histories. Read these works beyond their programme abstracts before claiming direct theoretical engagement.
